# HOTRANK Community / Submission Rules — V1 Draft

Submit work you are authorized to share. Include accurate title, source,
creator attribution, canonical attribution URL, AI tool/model information
where required, and the rights declaration. Do not submit unlawful, deceptive,
impersonating, hateful, abusive, unsafe, privacy-invasive, spam, duplicate, or
infringing material.

Every submission enters a private pending/review path. Deterministic checks may
return specific fixable reasons. Review can result in APPROVED,
NEEDS_CHANGES, REJECTED, or ESCALATED. Escalated work is not public and is not
eligible for ranking.

Ranking measures HOTRANK heat separately from moderation eligibility. A review
decision is not an artistic or popularity judgment. Creators may correct
fixable issues and resubmit through the supported product flow.
