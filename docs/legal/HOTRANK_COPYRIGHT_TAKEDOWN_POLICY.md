# HOTRANK Copyright / Takedown Policy — V1 Draft

HOTRANK respects copyright and related rights. A rights holder or authorized
representative may submit a takedown notice identifying the work, the allegedly
infringing material, the claimant’s contact details, the rights basis, and a
good-faith statement. Notices should be sent to the owner-designated legal
contact before launch.

HOTRANK may restrict, unpublish, or remove content while reviewing a notice.
Creators may submit a counter-notice where applicable. Repeat or deliberate
infringement may lead to account restrictions. Emergency safety, impersonation,
privacy, and legal requests may be handled under separate procedures.

The owner must add a monitored contact, jurisdiction-specific process, and
retention/appeal details before publishing this policy.
