# HOTRANK Privacy Policy — V1 Draft

Effective date: to be set by the owner before launch.

HOTRANK may process account identifiers supplied by Auth, profile details a
user chooses to publish, submission metadata, public interactions, security
events, and limited product analytics. Private prompts, creator notes, and
moderation records are not public content.

We use this information to authenticate users, operate account features,
review submissions, publish approved work, protect the service, and understand
aggregate product usage. We do not place passwords, tokens, OAuth codes,
cookies, prompt contents, or private moderation notes into analytics events.

Supabase Auth and the approved hosting/analytics providers may process data on
HOTRANK’s behalf. Users should receive the owner’s final retention, deletion,
regional-transfer, rights-request, and contact details before launch.

HOTRANK does not sell personal information. The owner must complete the final
legal review and publish a contact and rights-request process before enabling
broader creator onboarding.
