# HOTRANK Prompt Licence — V1 Draft

Prompt packages and workflows are separate from the original clip or artwork.
Where a prompt is marked public, HOTRANK may display the public prompt
metadata. Where it is locked or private, the prompt text remains protected by
the server-side publication boundary.

Any future prompt purchase would grant only the permitted use described at
checkout and in the applicable licence. It would not automatically grant
prompt redistribution, prompt resale, copyright in the original artwork or
video, collectible ownership, or commercial exploitation of the displayed
clip. Results are not guaranteed to reproduce the displayed work.

No payment provider or purchase flow is activated by this draft. Final pricing,
refund, tax, commercial-use, and consumer-law terms require owner and legal
approval before money movement.
